# Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?
Our client The Gaming Room currently only had a singular Android based game and wanted to expand into a multiplaform environment. It was our task to write up a software design document for a web based client server application called Draw it or Lose it. We had to support multiple players and teams conccurently, be accessable on all major browsers,operating systems, and mobile phones while utilizing a Java Backend. We also had to support memory and storage management to handle the loading of at least 200 images related to the game. 

# What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?
When developing this document I would often refer back to my work I had already completed to reference the material and ensure my points were in line with the expectations of the client. Making sure I didn't use complex terminology was also a concern, so I made sure to explain a complex piece of terminology if I used it as well. 

# What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?
The best part about having a design document is having structure. Eliminating questions you may have while coding a problem allows me to be confident that the feature I am working on is within the scope and needed.

# How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
By having meetings with the client and getting their expectations and requirements for the software. They came to us for our expertise and it is our job to translate exactly what they need based off of our conversations. I believe I did a good job in that regard.

# How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?
I used the Systems Development Life Cycle during this Design Document. I felt comfortable using this technique and would use it again. By finding out our requirements and constraints we get a good idea of the work we have laid out for us. After that we move onto UML/Domain modeling and then the infrastructure and platform reccomendations. Every step of the SDLC makes sense to me and from the perspective of a client, I would argue that it is extremely effective in allowing me to feel comfortable and satisfied with the end result before having my system coded and spending my allocated budget.
